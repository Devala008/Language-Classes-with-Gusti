Kenalan dulu— let's meet first

Hi, I'm Gusti — your teacher, not a textbook.

I've spent years teaching Bahasa Indonesia to travellers who want to actually connect — order food without pointing, chat with a driver, understand what's on the offering baskets outside every shop. Every course is planned by hand, session by session, around who's in the room.

Classes are small on purpose. That means real conversation from lesson one, corrections that make sense for how you speak, and a pace that never leaves beginners behind.

  • 01 Genuinely built for absolute beginners — first class assumes zero prior Indonesian.
  • 02 Small groups (max 4–5) or fully private 1:1 sessions, your choice.
  • 03 Practical, spoken-first Bahasa — the kind locals actually use in Bali.
  • 04 Flexible scheduling around your holiday or your week.
